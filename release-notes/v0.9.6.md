## English

### Quota for every provider a conversation uses (#187)

- The header shows the remaining quota of every provider the conversation has used, not only the current model's. A provider that is used up turns red and counts down to its reset, then checks again by itself.

### Wait for the reset, then continue (#188)

- New setting "When the usage limit is reached, wait for the reset and continue" (Settings › Conversations, off by default; each conversation can override it from its quota pill). A conversation that hits its limit shows a countdown ("Resumes automatically in 1h 12m") with Continue now and Cancel, and redoes the interrupted turn by itself once the limit resets. The server holds the wait, so it keeps going with the tab closed, and it respects a paused conversation.

## 中文

### 显示对话用过的每个提供商的额度（#187）

- 页眉现在会显示对话用过的每个提供商的剩余额度，而不只是当前模型的。额度用完的提供商会变成红色，显示距离重置的实时倒计时，到点后会自动重新查询。

### 等待额度重置后自动继续（#188）

- 新增设置“达到用量上限后，等待重置并自动继续”（在“设置 › 对话”里，默认关闭；每个对话都可以在额度标签里单独设置）。对话触达上限后会显示倒计时（“将在 1小时12分 后自动继续”），可点“立即继续”或“取消”；额度重置后会自动重做被中断的那一轮。等待由服务端保持，关闭标签页也会继续，并且遵守对话的暂停状态。
