## English

### Continue from here

- **Continue from here** (the clock button under a reply) goes back to that reply and goes on from it in the same conversation. The conversation keeps its place in the sidebar, its name, its pin and its settings; the whole conversation as it was is kept as a branch beside it (marked as a branch in the sidebar and listed in the side panel's Branches tab). *Branch from here* still forks a new conversation and leaves this one as it was.
- Until you send the next message, omp holds the new place only in memory: if the conversation's omp ends before that (the app quits, the conversation is ended), it opens at its old end again. Nothing is lost: the branch has the same messages.

### Faster CI

- The end-to-end runners are split by each test's median time over several CI runs. Tests that had been timed on a Mac (about a third of their time on CI's runners) made one runner a quarter slower than the others.

## 中文

### 从此处继续

- **“从此处继续”**（回复下方的时钟按钮）会回到那条回复，并在同一个对话里从那里接着进行。对话在侧边栏里的位置、名字、置顶和设置都保持不变；原来的整个对话作为一个分支留在旁边（侧边栏里标为分支，也列在侧边面板的“分支”标签页里）。“从此处分支”仍然会复制出一个新对话，原对话保持不变。
- 在你发出下一条消息之前，omp 只在内存里记着这个新位置：如果对话的 omp 在那之前结束（退出应用、结束对话），对话会重新从原来的末尾打开。不会丢失任何内容：分支里有同样的消息。

### 更快的 CI

- 端到端测试按每个测试在多次 CI 运行中的耗时中位数分配到各台运行器。以前有些测试的耗时是在 Mac 上测的（只有在 CI 运行器上的三分之一左右），导致其中一台运行器比其他的慢四分之一。
