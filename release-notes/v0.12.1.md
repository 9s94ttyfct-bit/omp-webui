## English

### Fixes

- The card that asks about a risky call says which setting asks. Guard dangerous commands' own question (**Ask me**) says it comes from the guard (Settings › Safety, or the conversation's own choice); after a reviewer's denial, or when no reviewer could be reached, the card's own block says why and there is no second note. Only the confirmation of a turn a remote page drives names Settings › Remote access. Without the extra note the denied card fits a phone again, **Cancel** included. The command's **Command:** and **In:** labels are in the page's language (#785).
- In the agent graph, an open batch's **Collapse** can be pressed again on a phone: it is drawn beside its frame, above the cards, and stays 24 px at any zoom (44 px on a touch screen where there is room), where it was a few pixels and, on a phone, under the batch's first card (#786).
- An off switch can be seen in light themes: its edge is now drawn in a colour of at least 3:1 against the card in every palette, light and dark (it was 1.2–2:1) (#787).
- The **Mode: Full** chip in New chat's **⋯** panel is named by what it shows ("Mode: Full"), so voice control reaches it by its words; what it is for stays its tooltip (#788).
- An open subagent's tokens, cost and time no longer run into each other or break a value over two lines in a narrow column (the agent graph on a phone): a figure that does not fit goes whole to a second row (#789).

## 中文

### 修复

- 询问高风险调用的卡片会说明是哪项设置在询问。“防护危险命令”自己的询问（**询问我**）说明来自防护（设置 › 安全，或这个对话自己的选择）；审查模型拒绝后，或联系不上审查模型时，卡片自己的说明块已讲清原因，不再多一段说明。只有远程页面驱动的这一轮的确认才提到 设置 › 远程访问。少了那段说明，拒绝卡片在手机上又能完整显示，**取消** 也在内。命令前原来的英文标签 “Command:” 和 “In:” 改为页面语言（“命令：”“运行目录：”）（#785）。
- 代理关系图里，打开的批次上的 **收起** 在手机上又能点了：它画在框的旁边、卡片之上，任何缩放下都是 24 像素（触屏上有空间时 44 像素）；原来只有几个像素，手机上还被批次第一张卡片盖住（#786）。
- 亮色主题下关着的开关看得见了：在每种配色的亮色和暗色下，开关边缘与卡片的对比度都至少 3:1（原来是 1.2–2:1）（#787）。
- 新对话 **⋯** 面板里的 **模式：完整** 按钮，其名称就是它显示的文字（“模式：完整”），语音控制按看到的词就能点到；它的用途仍在提示文字里（#788）。
- 打开的子代理的 token、费用和耗时在窄栏里（手机上的代理关系图）不再挤在一起，也不会把一个数字拆成两行：放不下的数字整个移到第二行（#789）。
