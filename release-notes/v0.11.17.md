## English

### Settings: grouped, and the list beside it stays in the window

- Settings' cards are in five groups, each under its name in the page and in the list beside it: **Look & layout** (Appearance, Sidebar and rail, Language, Keyboard shortcuts), **Conversations & files**, **Models & accounts**, **Approvals & safety** and **App & system**. Every card, its controls and what it does are unchanged; only the order is.
- The list beside the cards no longer runs off the bottom of the window when there are more cards than fit: it is as tall as the page at most, scrolls by itself, and keeps the card you are on in view while the page scrolls. Its rows are a little tighter. The row of chips on a narrow window is unchanged.

### Performance

- Not measured for this release: the benchmark gate (#645) needs an idle Mac for about an hour and is moved to v0.11.18. The first page load is 202.9 KiB of gzip JS (budget 228 KiB), as `bun run build` reports.

## 中文

### 设置：分组，旁边的目录不再超出窗口

- 设置里的卡片分成五组，页面里和旁边的目录里都在组名之下：**外观与布局**（外观、侧边栏与快捷栏、语言、键盘快捷键）、**对话与文件**、**模型与账号**、**审批与安全**、**应用与系统**。每张卡片、其中的控件和作用都没有变，只是顺序变了。
- 卡片多到放不下时，旁边的目录不再超出窗口底部：它最多和页面一样高，可以单独滚动，页面滚动时当前所在的卡片会保持在目录的可见范围内。目录的行距也稍微收紧了。窄窗口下顶部那一排标签不变。

### 性能

- 这一版没有做性能测量：基准门槛（#645）需要 Mac 空闲约一小时，已移到 v0.11.18。首屏 JS 为 202.9 KiB（gzip，预算 228 KiB），以 `bun run build` 的输出为准。
