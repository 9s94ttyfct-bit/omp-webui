## English

### A long goal run no longer slows down as it goes (#287, #295)

- What each step costs is now the same after thousands of steps as after the first few: the page adds new entries to what it already worked out instead of recomputing the whole conversation. In a 16-minute run on a 55 MB conversation, main-thread time per step stayed at 73–80 ms (it climbed from 99 to 137 ms before).
- A search result inside a very long turn now stays on screen: the steps around it remain visible (with "Show earlier steps" and "Show later steps"), and sending a message or using the scroll-to-bottom arrow returns to the latest steps.

### Send, reconnect and slow connections (#289, #290, #291)

- A message sent while omp is compacting the conversation or handing it off is no longer reported as failed after 45 s ("No answer from the server"). omp takes it when the compaction ends, so pressing Send again no longer sends it twice. If the server never receives the message, you still get the error and your draft back.
- After a dropped connection, a retried turn that omp recovered while you were away is shown as recovered, not still as a red error with a Retry button.
- A very long conversation now loads over a slow connection: large answers arrive in parts, so a slow but working connection is no longer mistaken for a dead one (a 24 MB conversation failed at 0.6 MB/s and now loads even at 0.25 MB/s).

### Fix: "Retrying…" that never retries (#249)

- omp 18.12 can lose a usage-limit retry: when two messages are queued during the wait and the wait is cancelled, omp reports that it is idle and never retries. The conversation no longer counts down to a retry that won't come; the failed turn keeps its own Retry button.
- Cancel and Stop now end a wait omp left behind, and Retry now no longer leaves the conversation marked as working.

### Settings, accessibility and layout (#288, #292, #294, #296, #297)

- Settings: pressing Tab onto a mode, appearance or palette option no longer scrolls the sidebar and header out of view.
- Screen readers now announce the Session settings and quota popovers by their titles.
- Usage on phones: the "By project" and "By agent type" cards fit the screen, scroll their table inside, and keep column headers on one line.
- Closing the header's Rename dialog (Esc, Cancel or Save) returns keyboard focus to the pencil button, and so do other dialogs that open on a text field.
- The "Recommended" install badge and the other accent badges are now readable in every palette, light and dark.

### Other fixes (#293, #298, #299, #300, #301, #302, #303)

- Usage's daily cost days are now labelled with the UTC date they cover (they were a day early west of UTC), and the "since" date says UTC too.
- A new chat no longer logs 404 errors in the console.
- Search highlights the match for one- and two-character queries, Chinese words included, and shows … where the text is cut.
- Errors from the server about offline mode and omp updates now read in your language and no longer show internal setting names.
- Simplified Chinese now uses one word for "account" everywhere.
- Send feedback now also removes Google tokens (`ya29.`), Slack webhook links, `curl -u` logins, passwords written in sentences, and private keys.
- With Settings open in two tabs, a tab could occasionally keep showing an old offline setting (a stale MCP checkbox) after the other tab changed it, and its next save could undo that change. Each tab now keeps the newest state.

## 中文

### 长时间运行的 goal 不再越跑越慢（#287、#295）

- 每一步的开销在几千步之后与最初几步相同：页面把新条目接到已算好的结果上，不再每次重算整段对话。在 55 MB 的对话上连续运行 16 分钟，每一步的主线程耗时保持在 73–80 毫秒（修复前从 99 毫秒涨到 137 毫秒）。
- 在很长的回合中点击搜索结果后，命中的内容会一直停留在屏幕上：前后的步骤保持显示（可点“显示更早的步骤”“显示更晚的步骤”），发送消息或点底部的“滚动到底部”箭头则回到最新步骤。

### 发送、重连与慢速网络（#289、#290、#291）

- omp 正在压缩或交接对话时发送的消息，不再在 45 秒后误报失败（“服务器 45 秒内没有响应”）。压缩结束后 omp 会收下这条消息，所以不会再因为重复点“发送”而发出两遍。如果服务器根本没收到消息，仍会提示错误并退回草稿。
- 连接断开期间，如果 omp 已经通过重试恢复了失败的回合，重新连上后会显示“重试 N 次后已恢复”，不再显示带“重试”按钮的红色错误。
- 超长对话现在在慢速网络下也能加载：大的回复分块到达，慢但正常的连接不再被误判为断线（24 MB 的对话在 0.6 MB/s 下曾无法加载，现在 0.25 MB/s 也能加载）。

### 修复：一直显示“正在重试…”却不重试（#249）

- omp 18.12 在某种情况下会丢掉用量上限的重试：等待期间排队了两条消息、又取消了等待时，omp 会报告自己已空闲，之后再也不重试。现在对话不再为这种不会发生的重试倒计时，失败的那一轮保留自己的“重试”按钮。
- “取消”和“停止”现在能结束 omp 遗留的等待；点“立即重试”后，对话也不再一直显示为工作中。

### 设置、无障碍与布局（#288、#292、#294、#296、#297）

- 设置：用 Tab 键移到模式、外观或配色选项时，不再把侧边栏和标题栏滚出屏幕。
- 屏幕阅读器现在会按标题朗读“本会话设置”和额度详情弹窗。
- 手机上的用量页：“按项目”和“按代理类型”卡片适配屏幕宽度，表格在卡片内横向滚动，列标题不再换行。
- 关闭标题栏的“重命名”对话框（Esc、取消或保存）后，键盘焦点回到铅笔按钮；其他以文本框开头的对话框也一样。
- “推荐”安装徽标和其他强调色徽标，在所有配色的浅色和深色模式下都清晰可读。

### 其他修复（#293、#298、#299、#300、#301、#302、#303）

- 用量页每日费用按它所覆盖的 UTC 日期标注（此前在 UTC 以西的时区会早一天），“自……起”的日期也注明 UTC。
- 新对话不再在控制台里出现 404 错误。
- 搜索一两个字的关键词（包括中文词语）时也会高亮匹配，并在文本被截断处显示 …。
- 服务器返回的离线模式和 omp 更新错误现在用你选的语言显示，不再出现内部设置名。
- 简体中文里 “account” 统一称为“账号”。
- 发送反馈时还会移除 Google 令牌（`ya29.`）、Slack Webhook 链接、`curl -u` 登录信息、写在句子里的密码和私钥。
- 在两个标签页里同时打开设置时，一个标签页偶尔会在另一个标签页修改后仍显示旧的离线设置（过期的 MCP 勾选），它的下一次保存还可能撤销对方的修改。现在每个标签页都只保留最新的状态。
