## English

### Send feedback is in Settings

- **Send feedback moved from the main sidebar to Settings**, where it is in view on every page: on a wide window it is under the pages' names in the Settings index on the left; on a phone or a narrow window, where that index is a row at the top, it is a highlighted box at the foot of every Settings page ("Something broken, or a feature you want? Tell us."). The main sidebar no longer has it. The Feedback card in Settings › App & system, the command palette and the keyboard shortcuts card still open it too.

## 中文

### “发送反馈”移到了设置里

- **“发送反馈”从主侧边栏移到了设置里**，在设置的每一页都看得到：宽窗口里，它在左侧设置目录的页面名称下方；手机或窄窗口里（目录变成顶部的一行），它是每个设置页底部一个醒目的方框（“遇到问题，或想要新功能？告诉我们。”）。主侧边栏里不再有它。设置 › 应用与系统里的“反馈”卡片、命令面板和键盘快捷键卡片也仍然可以打开它。
