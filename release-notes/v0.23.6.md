## English

### The agent's recommended answers are shown again

- **A question the agent asks with preselected answers shows them.** When the agent asks with a form and preselects the option it recommends, each choice in the question card now has a radio mark: the recommended option's mark is filled, the option is outlined and marked **recommended**. A click on any option still answers at once.
- **Multiple-choice questions show what is ticked.** With omp 18.19 and later, the ticks of a checkbox question could be missing or wrong; the card now shows them, as omp 18.20 reports them.
- **A question's explanation is shown once, in its paragraphs.** With omp 18.19 and later, the text around a question appeared twice — once run together in bold, once below it. It now appears once, with its paragraph breaks.

## 中文

### 重新显示代理推荐的答案

- **代理预选了答案的问题会显示出来。** 代理用表单提问并预选它推荐的选项时，问题卡片里每个选项前都有一个单选圆点：推荐的那项圆点填满、加边框，并标出 **推荐**。点任何一个选项仍会立即作答。
- **多选题显示已勾选的选项。** 使用 omp 18.19 及更高版本时，多选题的勾选可能不显示或显示错误；现在卡片会按 omp 18.20 发来的状态显示。
- **问题的说明只显示一次，并保留分段。** 使用 omp 18.19 及更高版本时，问题周围的说明会出现两次：一次连成一段粗体，一次在下面。现在只显示一次，并保留段落。
