## English

### Isolated environment after a conversation started

- **A running conversation can switch its folder's isolated environment.** The switch New chat has is now also in the conversation's *This session* panel, under *Advanced*. As in New chat it is the folder's setting: every conversation in that folder follows. Switched from your own page only.
- **It applies when the conversation restarts.** A running omp keeps the environment it started with, so the conversation is restarted on the same session to apply it. While it is idle, the panel offers *Restart now* (and asks once: a restart ends what the conversation started, such as background jobs and dev servers). While it is replying it is left to finish: the header says *Restart to apply*, and a click on it restarts the conversation when you choose. A paused conversation is resumed first.
- What was installed outside the project before the switch stays where it is; only what is installed after the restart goes into `.ompw-env`.

## 中文

### 对话开始后也能开启独立环境

- **正在运行的对话可以切换所在文件夹的独立环境。** 新对话里的那个开关，现在也出现在对话的“本次会话”面板的“高级”里。和新对话里一样，这是文件夹的设置：同一文件夹里的所有对话都跟着变。只能在你自己的页面上切换。
- **重启对话后生效。** 正在运行的 omp 保留启动时的环境，所以要在同一会话上重启对话才会生效。对话空闲时，面板会提供“现在重启”（会先确认一次：重启会结束对话启动的后台任务、开发服务器等）。正在回复时不会打断：标题栏显示“重启后生效”，由你决定何时点它重启。已暂停的对话要先继续。
- 切换之前装到项目外面的东西不会被搬进来；只有重启之后安装的内容才会进 `.ompw-env`。
