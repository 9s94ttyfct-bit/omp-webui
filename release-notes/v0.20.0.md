## English

### Status line and chat header: as much as fits

- **The status line under the message box and the chat header no longer fold everything at once below one width.** Each gives up one thing at a time, least useful first, only as far as the room it has needs, and takes it back when the room is there again (with a little to spare, so a window right at the edge does not flicker). A wide column with the side panel open now shows the working time, the model, the folder, the branch, the cost and the context, where it showed only the branch.
- **Status line order**: the folder is shortened and then left to the header (which then names it in full), the working time, the goal and the cost move to the header's ⋯ menu, the branch name is shortened and then shown as its icon (the name stays in its tooltip), and last the model name ends in an ellipsis. The context, the agents running, the usage-limit warning and the pull request always stay. The line is always one row.
- **Header order**: Rename, Export, Loaded, the cost, Goal and then This session move into the ⋯ menu, and only then is the quota written shorter. The title keeps its room until nothing else can give way. The ⋯ menu appears only while it holds something, and lists exactly what the header and the line are not showing.

### Plugins: updates, automatic updates, a clearer page

- **The Plugins page opens on an Overview**: how many plugins and skills you have (on and off), your marketplaces, and how many updates are waiting; each count opens its tab. The health check is there too. The tabs are now Overview, Plugins, Skills, Marketplaces, Discover and MCP servers.
- **Updates**: *Check now* refreshes your marketplaces' catalogs, then finds newer versions of your marketplace plugins, npm plugins and registry skills, yours and those of the projects you recently worked in. Each shows where it is installed and its version, old → new. Pick some with the checkboxes and *Update selected*, or *Update all*: they are installed one after another in one job, a failure does not stop the rest, and open conversations reload their plugins once. A plugin installed from git, a URL or a folder is listed as not checked, since only reinstalling it can tell.
- **Update automatically** (off unless you turn it on): checks a minute after omp-webui starts and every 6 hours, and installs everything newer once no conversation is working, asking, paused or waiting. The section shows the next check, what it is waiting for, and the last automatic update.
- **Marketplaces update in bulk**: tick several and *Update selected*, or *Update all*.

## 中文

### 状态栏和对话标题栏：放得下多少就显示多少

- **输入框下的状态栏和对话标题栏不再在某个宽度以下一次性全部折叠。** 空间不够时按从次要到重要的顺序一项一项让出位置，只让到放得下为止；空间回来时再一项一项恢复（留有少量余量，窗口正好卡在边界时不会来回闪）。打开侧边面板的宽栏里，现在能看到工作时间、模型、文件夹、分支、费用和上下文，以前只剩分支。
- **状态栏的让位顺序**：文件夹先缩短，再交给标题栏（此时标题栏显示完整路径）；工作时间、目标和费用移到标题栏的 ⋯ 菜单；分支名先缩短，再只显示图标（名称保留在提示里）；最后模型名以省略号结尾。上下文、正在运行的代理、用量上限提醒和拉取请求始终保留。状态栏始终只占一行。
- **标题栏的让位顺序**：重命名、导出、已加载、费用、目标、本会话依次移到 ⋯ 菜单，然后才缩短额度的写法。标题会保留它的空间，直到没有别的可以让。⋯ 菜单只在有内容时出现，列出的正好是标题栏和状态栏没显示的那些。

### 插件：更新、自动更新、更清楚的页面

- **插件页面先打开“概览”**：你有多少插件和技能（开启 / 关闭）、多少插件市场、有多少可更新；点任一数字打开对应标签页。健康检查也在这里。标签页现在是概览、插件、技能、插件市场、发现和 MCP 服务器。
- **更新**：“立即检查”先刷新插件市场的目录，再查找你的插件市场插件、npm 插件和注册表技能的新版本，包括你的和最近工作过的项目里的。每项显示安装位置和“旧版本 → 新版本”。勾选后“更新所选”，或“全部更新”：在一个任务里依次安装，某一项失败不影响其余，打开的对话只重新加载一次插件。从 git、URL 或文件夹安装的插件列为“未检查”，因为只有重新安装才知道有没有新版本。
- **自动更新**（默认关闭）：omp-webui 启动一分钟后检查一次，之后每 6 小时一次；没有对话在工作、提问、暂停或等待时，自动安装所有新版本。这一栏会显示下次检查时间、正在等待什么，以及上次自动更新的结果。
- **插件市场可以批量更新**：勾选几个后“更新所选”，或“全部更新”。
