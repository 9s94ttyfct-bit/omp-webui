## English

### Passkeys in the built-in browser

- **Signing in with a passkey no longer closes the app.** When a page in the built-in browser asked for a passkey, macOS ended the whole app. Now the passkey window opens: scan its QR code with your phone or tablet, or use a USB security key. If macOS asks whether omp-webui may use Bluetooth, allow it: the phone is reached over Bluetooth.
- **Passkeys saved in this Mac's own iCloud Keychain are not offered.** Apple lets a browser use them for every site only with a permission it grants to an organisation's developer account after review, which this app does not have.

### Advisor

- **Turning the advisor on or off for a conversation now holds for the very next turn, also on a slow computer.** The switch used to count a fixed moment for omp to take the change; when omp took longer, a turn sent right after was still reviewed (or not) as before. Now the switch waits until omp says the change is in force.

## 中文

### 内置浏览器里的通行密钥（passkey）

- **用通行密钥登录不会再让应用退出了。** 以前内置浏览器里的网页一请求通行密钥，macOS 就会结束整个应用。现在会打开通行密钥窗口：用手机或平板扫描其中的二维码，或者使用 USB 安全密钥。如果 macOS 询问 omp-webui 能否使用蓝牙，请允许，因为要通过蓝牙连接手机。
- **不提供这台 Mac 自己的 iCloud 钥匙串里的通行密钥。** Apple 只允许拥有其专门权限的浏览器为所有网站使用这些密钥，这个权限要由组织开发者账号申请并经过审核，本应用没有。

### 顾问（advisor）

- **为对话打开或关闭顾问后，紧接着的下一轮就会按新设置执行，在较慢的电脑上也一样。** 以前开关只等一段固定的时间就认为 omp 已经生效；omp 用得更久时，紧接着发出的那一轮仍会按原来的设置被复查（或不被复查）。现在开关会等到 omp 确认新设置已经生效。
