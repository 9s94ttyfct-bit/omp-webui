## English

### Semantic search

- **Find conversations by what they are about.** Settings › Conversations › *Semantic search* (off until you turn it on). The sidebar's search then also lists, under **Related** after *In messages*, the conversations closest in meaning to what you typed: a search for "fruit picking" finds the one about the apple harvest. A click opens the conversation at the message that matched.
- **Everything runs on this computer.** Turning it on downloads llama.cpp's official `llama-server` and Google's EmbeddingGemma 2 model (about 330 MB, both checked against pinned sizes and SHA-256). The model runs here, on the GPU when there is one (Metal on Apple silicon, Vulkan on Windows and Linux, the processor otherwise). No conversation or search is sent anywhere.
- **It stays out of the way.** Nothing is indexed while a conversation is working: what was written meanwhile is indexed once none is. The model is unloaded a minute after its last use (about 170 MB while loaded on an Apple M4 Pro). The newest messages are found under *In messages* until they are indexed.
- **Turn off and delete** stops it and removes everything it downloaded and made. Only your own page (this computer's, or one signed in through Cloudflare Access) can turn it on or off.

## 中文

### 语义搜索

- **按对话的内容找对话。** 设置 › 对话 › “语义搜索”（打开之前不起作用）。打开后，侧边栏的搜索还会在“消息中”之后列出**语义相关**的对话：搜“摘水果”能找到讲苹果采收的那个。点一行会在匹配的那条消息处打开对话。
- **全部在这台电脑上运行。** 打开时会下载 llama.cpp 官方的 `llama-server` 和 Google 的 EmbeddingGemma 2 模型（约 330 MB，都按固定的大小和 SHA-256 校验）。模型在本机运行，有 GPU 就用 GPU（Apple 芯片用 Metal，Windows 和 Linux 用 Vulkan，否则用处理器）。不发送任何对话内容或搜索词。
- **不打扰正在进行的工作。** 有对话在工作时不建索引，等没有对话在工作时再把期间写下的内容加进来。模型最后一次使用 1 分钟后就会卸载（在 Apple M4 Pro 上加载后约占 170 MB）。最新的消息在加进索引之前，可以在“消息中”找到。
- **“关闭并删除”** 会停止它，并删掉它下载和生成的一切。只有你自己的页面（这台电脑上的，或通过 Cloudflare Access 登录的）能打开或关闭它。
