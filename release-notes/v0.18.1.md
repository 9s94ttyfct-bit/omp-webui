## English

### The Mac app and its browser

- Installing, updating or clearing the built-in browser no longer leaves the app stuck. The app used to stop answering a quit after the install and had to be force quit. Now it quits and opens again by itself, with the browser ready.
- An app started on another data folder (`OMP_WEBUI_DATA_DIR`) comes back on that folder after the browser's restart, not on the default one.
- Closing a tab in the Browser tab no longer asks whether to quit omp-webui, and no longer makes the window disappear or the app quit. The tab closes and the others stay as they were.
- A page that was not on screen when its tab was closed now really closes. Before, it stayed open until the app quit, and it slowed that quit down.

### Chat header

- The title of a conversation is no longer squeezed out of sight. When the usage figures (several providers' quota, the cost) and the buttons do not leave the title room, the header moves them into its menu, as it does in a narrow window.

## 中文

### Mac 应用与内置浏览器

- 安装、更新或清除内置浏览器后，应用不再卡住。以前装完后应用不再响应退出，只能强制结束；现在它会自动退出并重新打开，浏览器随即可用。
- 用另一个数据文件夹（`OMP_WEBUI_DATA_DIR`）启动的应用，在浏览器重启后会回到同一个文件夹，而不是默认文件夹。
- 在浏览器标签页里关闭一个标签，不会再弹出“是否退出 omp-webui”的询问，也不会让窗口消失或应用退出。标签直接关闭，其他标签保持不变。
- 关闭标签时没有显示在屏幕上的页面，现在会真正关闭。以前它一直开着，直到应用退出，还会拖慢退出。

### 对话标题栏

- 对话标题不会再被挤得看不见。用量数字（多个服务商的额度、费用）和按钮放不下标题时，标题栏会把它们收进菜单，和窗口较窄时一样。
