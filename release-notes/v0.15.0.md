## English

### Live animations that follow the agent (optional, off by default)

- Settings › Look & layout › Appearance has a new switch, **Live animations**, with an **Intensity** of Subtle, Normal or Lively. Both follow you to every browser and port, like your other preferences (`ompw.animations`, `ompw.animationIntensity`), and the settings search finds them ("animation", in English or Chinese).
- While the model writes a reply, a band of light sweeps over that reply and over the turn's running time.
- A running tool card has a bar moving along its bottom edge until the tool is done.
- On the agent graph, particles run along the lines as it happens: down a *Started* line when an agent starts a subagent, along a *Message* line when one agent writes to another, and back up in green when a subagent finishes well.
- When a turn ends well, or a goal is completed, a short burst rises from the bottom of the conversation (a goal's is bigger). A failed, stopped or retried turn gets none, and nor does a conversation you only open after it ended.
- If your system asks for reduced motion, the animations stay off whatever is chosen, and Settings says so.
- Nothing moves while the agent is idle: every effect exists only while what it follows runs, and the agent graph has no animation loop. With animations on, an idle page runs no animation and asks for no animation frame; while a tool runs they add no repaints and well under 1% of a CPU core (measurements in the technical documentation).

## 中文

### 跟随智能体工作的实时动画（可选，默认关闭）

- 设置 › 外观与布局 › 外观 新增开关**实时动画**，以及**强度**：轻微、正常或活泼。两者和其他偏好一样会跟着你到每个浏览器和端口（`ompw.animations`、`ompw.animationIntensity`），设置搜索也能找到（“动画”“animation”）。
- 模型输出回复时，一道微光扫过这条回复和这一轮的已运行时间。
- 运行中的工具卡片底边有一条移动的进度条，工具结束即消失。
- 代理图上，事情发生时会有粒子沿连线流动：智能体启动子代理时沿“启动”连线向下，一个智能体给另一个发消息时沿“消息”连线，子代理顺利完成时以绿色沿连线返回。
- 一轮对话顺利结束或目标完成时，对话底部会升起一阵短暂的庆祝（目标的更大）。失败、被停止或重试的轮次没有；对话结束后才打开它也不会有。
- 系统设置了“减少动态效果”时，无论这里怎么选，动画都保持关闭，设置里会说明这一点。
- 智能体空闲时什么都不动：每个动效只在它跟随的事情运行时存在，代理图也没有动画循环。打开动画后，空闲页面没有运行中的动画，也不请求任何动画帧；工具运行时它们不增加重绘，占用远低于一个 CPU 核心的 1%（测量数据见技术文档）。
