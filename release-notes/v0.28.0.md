## English

### Disk space

- **A new page, Disk space, shows what each project takes on disk.** It lists every folder of the projects folder, and every other folder conversations ran in, with the size of the folder, how many conversations ran there and what they take (each transcript with its subagent transcripts and kept files), the total, and when it was last used. The header shows the disk's free space. A click on a column sorts by it, and the sort is remembered. The sizes are measured in the background, one folder at a time and at a lower priority, and kept across restarts; opening the page measures again when the last measurement is older than ten minutes, and **Refresh** does so at once. It is in the sidebar (and the rail) under *Usage & cost*, at `#/disk`; a bar you arranged before gets it at the end of its group.
- **Clean up… for each folder.** The dialog lists the folder's conversations, to delete the ones you tick, and offers the folder itself, or only its dependency folders (`node_modules`, `.venv`, `target`, …), to move to the Trash. The two parts are independent, and the button says what will happen and how much room it frees. Here only what is alive keeps a folder: a conversation running in it (unless you are deleting that conversation), a Terminal tab or a program working in it, or a sidebar project whose folder it is. Conversations that only ran there do not; the dialog says how many of them stay. Everything the delete dialogs already refuse is refused here too: the home and system folders, omp's data, a folder holding another project, and a path that changed since the dialog looked at it. Moving to the Trash needs this computer's page, or a page allowed to send files here.

## 中文

### 磁盘空间

- **新增“磁盘空间”页面，显示每个项目占用了多少磁盘。** 它列出项目文件夹里的每个文件夹，以及对话运行过的其他文件夹，显示文件夹大小、在这里运行过多少对话及其占用（每条记录连同它的子代理记录和保留的文件）、合计和最近使用时间。页头显示磁盘的剩余空间。点列标题按该列排序，排序方式会被记住。大小在后台测量，一次一个文件夹，优先级较低，重启后仍然保留；打开页面时如果上次测量已超过十分钟会重新测量，点“刷新”则立即测量。入口在侧边栏（以及快捷栏）“用量与费用”下面，地址是 `#/disk`；你以前调整过的栏会把它加在所在分组的末尾。
- **每个文件夹都有“清理…”。** 对话框列出这个文件夹的对话，勾选的会被删除；还提供把文件夹本身、或只把其中的依赖文件夹（`node_modules`、`.venv`、`target` 等）移到废纸篓。两部分互不影响，按钮会写明将要做什么、能释放多少空间。在这里，只有“活着”的东西才会挡住移动文件夹：正在其中运行的对话（除非你正在删除它）、在其中工作的终端标签页或程序，或者以它为文件夹的侧边栏项目。只是曾经在这里运行过的对话不会挡住；对话框会说明有多少个对话会保留下来。删除对话框原本拒绝的情况这里同样拒绝：主目录和系统目录、omp 的数据、包含其他项目的文件夹，以及对话框查看之后已经变了的路径。移到废纸篓需要在这台电脑自己的页面上操作，或者是允许向这里发送文件的页面。
