## English

### The macOS app opens again

- **0.27.0 closed at once on every launch on a Mac that shows another Mac's notifications** (the Macs menu, *Notifications* on for a Mac). The app read that Mac's sign-in before anything in it had set up WebKit, and WebKit's answer crashed it. It now sets WebKit up first. Nothing else changed: install 0.27.1 over 0.27.0 or 0.26.0, and the Macs, their sign-ins and their notification choices are kept.

## 中文

### macOS 应用又能打开了

- **在显示其他 Mac 通知的 Mac 上（“Mac”菜单里某台 Mac 开着“通知”），0.27.0 每次启动都会立即退出。** 应用在 WebKit 还没初始化时就去读取那台 Mac 的登录信息，WebKit 返回结果时让应用崩溃了。现在会先初始化 WebKit。其他没有变化：直接把 0.27.1 装在 0.27.0 或 0.26.0 上即可，Mac 列表、登录状态和通知设置都会保留。
