## English

### Goals

- **A goal omp has finished no longer stays on screen, and Drop always works.** When omp completed a goal (or it was dropped elsewhere) the goal bar could keep showing it, as *Pausing* or *Paused*, read from an older part of the transcript; Pause and Drop then did nothing, because omp had no goal left to act on. A conversation whose omp is running now shows only the goal omp holds, and when omp answers a click with "no goal" the bar goes away. A goal set in the terminal still shows when the conversation is opened, before omp runs for it here.

## 中文

### 目标

- **omp 已经结束的目标不再一直挂在界面上，“放弃目标”总能生效。** omp 完成了目标（或在别处放弃了目标）之后，目标栏可能还从对话记录里较早的部分读出这个目标，显示为“正在暂停”或“已暂停”；这时点“暂停”或“放弃”都没有反应，因为 omp 手里已经没有目标可以操作。现在，正在运行 omp 的对话只显示 omp 自己持有的目标；omp 回答“没有目标”时，目标栏随即消失。在终端里设的目标，打开对话、这里还没运行 omp 时，依然会显示。
