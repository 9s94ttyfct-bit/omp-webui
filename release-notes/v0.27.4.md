## English

### omp 19.1

- **The `ask` tool's question shows again.** omp 19.1 sends the whole question form at once instead of one field at a time, and omp-webui did not recognize it: nothing appeared and the agent waited for an answer forever. The form now appears in the conversation as it was written: its text, lists, code, tables and pictures, choices with an *Other…* box, drop-downs and text fields, filled in the way the agent filled them. *Submit answers* first checks the answers by omp's own rules (required fields, number ranges, lengths, email addresses, links, patterns) and marks what needs fixing; *Skip* declines the question. When omp's time limit runs out, omp submits the form as it was first filled in, and the card says so. Older omp still asks field by field, as before.
- **No more notice listing mounted tools.** omp 19.1 reports every MCP tool it makes available each time a conversation starts; that notice is no longer shown, as the older one was not.
- **Guard › Benchmark asks its reviewer with no tools at all.** omp 19.1 offered the reviewer its bug-report tool even without tools; it is now turned off for those requests.
- Questions filed in unattended mode, the docs tool and read-only plan mode were checked against omp 19.1 and work as before.

## 中文

### omp 19.1

- **`ask` 工具的提问重新显示了。** omp 19.1 改为把整张提问表单一次发来，不再一个字段一个字段地问，omp-webui 认不出这种新请求，结果什么都不显示，代理一直等着回答。现在表单会按代理写的样子显示在对话里：说明文字、列表、代码、表格和图片，带“其他…”输入框的选项、下拉选择和文本框，初始值都按代理填好。点“提交回答”会先按 omp 自己的规则检查（必填、数字范围、长度、邮箱、网址、格式），标出需要改的地方；点“跳过”放弃这次提问。omp 的时限到了，omp 会按表单最初填好的内容提交，卡片上会提示这一点。旧版 omp 仍按原来的方式逐个字段询问。
- **不再弹出“已挂载工具”的提示。** omp 19.1 每次开始对话都会报告它提供了哪些 MCP 工具；这条提示现在不再显示，和以前的旧提示一样。
- **“防护 › 基准测试”问审查模型时不给任何工具。** omp 19.1 即使在不带工具时也会给审查模型提供它的问题报告工具；这些请求里现在把它关掉了。
- 无人值守模式下记下的问题、文档工具和只读计划模式都在 omp 19.1 上检查过，和以前一样能用。
