## English

### Conversations and the sidebar

- Conversations that are working now (running, waiting for your answer, or held by the quota floor) sort to the top of **Today** while they run, and go back to their place when they stop (#699).
- The **Pinned** heading folds and unfolds the group, and says how many it hides while folded. It is remembered (#700).
- A button at the right of the list's first line chooses the order: **Last activity** (as before), **Created** or **Name A–Z**. The choice is remembered; pinned conversations come first in every order, then the working ones; a search keeps its own ranking (#701).
- Every assistant reply and every tool call shows the clock time it started, `14:32:07`, with the date before it when it is not today. It is the same live and in a saved conversation, in both languages, and does not move the layout (#691).
- The quick prompts button (⚡) is hidden while something is typed in the message box, so it no longer sits beside *Steer*, which has the same icon. Its shortcuts and the command menu's entries keep working (#702).

### Right panel: Questions, Worktrees, Guidance

- **Questions** moves from the sidebar and the rail to a tab of the right panel, the last of the top row. It shows the conversation's own questions with the count of those open, and its button *All conversations' questions* opens the full Questions page, which is also under *Go to* in the command palette and in the macOS menu bar icon's menu (#693).
- A **Worktrees** tab, the last of the lower row, lists the repository's `git worktree`s read only: branch, what is not committed in each, and how many of its commits are not on the main branch. On a phone, the sheet's row of thirteen tabs scrolls sideways (#698).
- Model guidance has its own **Guidance** tab in the right panel instead of a row of the Info tab (#704).
- The Terminal tab has **Clear**, and ⌘K does the same while the screen has the keyboard (#696).
- The Issues tab refreshes by itself about every minute and when the window comes back; what you typed in its search stays (#695).

### Unattended mode

- Answering a question while its conversation is working: choose **Queue after the turn** (as before, the default) or **Steer in now**. An idle conversation starts a turn either way (#703).
- A conversation that compacts while a question waits still knows it asked it: under its instruction it is told the questions it filed that still wait, so it does not file them again. An answer that comes turns later says when the question was filed and what the agent said it would do meanwhile, and asks it to square the answer with any other way it has gone since (#694).

### Attach PDF and Word

- A PDF or a `.docx` dropped, pasted or chosen with Attach goes into the message as the text read from it, in the browser. The pages of a scanned PDF are sent as images. Limits and what was left out are said in the message box, in the text the model reads and in a toast. In the Files tab a PDF opens as its pages and a Word document as its text (#697).

### Session info, usage and quota

- The Info tab's token and cost figures are the whole conversation's: its own requests including the ones omp makes on the side (a judge's, the guard reviewer's), its subagents' and its advisors', also across compactions. They are taken from the transcripts; omp is asked only for the message and tool-call counts (#687).
- **Time worked** in the Info tab: how long the agent has worked on the conversation in all, subagents included, the same live and after a reload (#692).
- The header's quota covers every provider the conversation spends: its model's, its subagents' and advisors', and the guard reviewer's (#688).
- Usage & cost › **By agent type** lists the guard reviewer's spend as its own `guard` type, with tokens and cost; the totals stay omp's and the reviewer is counted once (#689). Known gap (#706): a guard request in a stretch of a transcript that `omp stats` never ingested is moved all the same, so the types can differ from omp's total by a few requests.

### macOS menu bar icon

- The number beside the icon is what waits for you: conversations at a dialog plus open unattended questions. A **✓** says a run finished since you last looked (opening the menu or bringing the app's window to the front). The menu adds *N questions waiting*, which opens the window on the Questions page, and *N conversations finished*. It works with the app in the background (#705).

### Guard

- The reviewer's fixed instructions now leave read-only looks to your rules: omp's usage statistics (`~/.omp/stats.db` opened read-only), transcripts and other files of omp's agent folder, and a local `/api` request that only reads. Changing the app's settings, approvals or the guard, and reading the guard's own files, settings or rules (also through `/api/guard`) or the sign-in token, are still always blocked.

### Fixes

- Pinning from the sidebar could fork the conversation instead of pinning it: the pin was made by an omp that resumed the conversation, and when another process held it, that omp's start-up write went to a new sibling file, which was pinned instead. It is now made by a short-lived omp that never resumes the conversation (#686).
- Info tab: the token and cost figures were wrong for a conversation with subagents or compactions (#687, above).
- Usage & cost: the cache rate of a row where omp sends none showed 0%. It is worked out from the row's tokens, and `–` where nothing was read (#689).
- Unattended questions: an answer that arrives after a compaction or many turns later no longer reads as if nothing had happened since (#694, above).

### Performance

- The Usage & cost page shows its figures at once: the server keeps omp's last `omp stats` answer, the page draws it with a line saying when it is from, and the new figures replace it when omp has brought its own database up to date. Measured here, a cold `omp stats` takes 12.2 s, nearly all of it omp syncing its transcripts, and a warm one 0.1–0.4 s (#690).
- Dates and times are formatted with one formatter per language and shape instead of a new one on every call. The bench gate (v0.11.18 against this release) had found the first page load and revealing a long conversation's history slower: the clock time now on every reply and tool call, and the sidebar's date headings, each built a formatter, about 15 µs a time. With the fix, a CPU profile of the bench's conversations spends less JavaScript than v0.11.18 on the first load (about 85 ms against 108 ms) and on opening the long conversation (about 77 ms against 86 ms). The gate run after the fix found nothing worse, but it is **provisional** (the machine was busy) and reported drift between its two runs of v0.11.18, so it proves nothing on its own.

## 中文

### 对话与侧边栏

- 正在工作的对话（运行中、在等你回答、或被额度下限暂缓）在运行期间排在**今天**的最上面，停下后回到原来的位置（#699）。
- “已置顶”标题可以折叠和展开这一组，折叠时会说隐藏了几个，并且会被记住（#700）。
- 列表第一行右侧的按钮可以选择排序：**最近活动**（和以前一样）、**创建时间**或**名称 A–Z**。选择会被记住；不论哪种排序，置顶的对话都在最前，接着是正在工作的对话；搜索保持它自己的排序（#701）。
- 每条助手回复和每次工具调用都显示它开始的时刻，`14:32:07`，不是今天时前面带日期。实时的和已保存的对话里一样，中英文都有，不会让版面跳动（#691）。
- 输入框里有文字时隐藏快速指令按钮（⚡），它不再和图标相同的“立刻插入”并排。快捷键和命令菜单里的条目照常可用（#702）。

### 右侧面板：问题、工作树、模型指引

- **问题**从侧边栏和窄栏移到右侧面板的一个标签页，是上面一行的最后一个。它显示这个对话自己的问题和待回答的数量，它的“所有对话的问题”按钮打开完整的“问题”页；那一页也在命令面板的“前往”里，以及 macOS 菜单栏图标的菜单里（#693）。
- 新增**工作树**标签页（下面一行的最后一个），只读地列出仓库的每个 `git worktree`：分支、其中未提交的内容，以及有多少个提交不在主分支上。在手机上，浮层里十三个标签页这一行可以左右滚动（#698）。
- 模型指引有了自己的**模型指引**标签页，不再是“信息”标签页里的一行（#704）。
- 终端标签页有了**清屏**，终端拿着键盘焦点时 ⌘K 也一样（#696）。
- Issues 标签页会自己刷新：大约每分钟一次，窗口回到前台时也会；你在搜索框里输入的内容会保留（#695）。

### 无人值守模式

- 在对话正忙时回答问题：可以选**排队，本轮结束后处理**（和以前一样，默认）或**立即插入（steer）**。对话空闲时，不论怎么选都开始新一轮（#703）。
- 在问题还在等待时压缩的对话，仍然知道自己问过：在它的说明下面，会告诉它记下的、仍在等待的问题，所以不会再记一遍。过了好几轮才到的回答会写明问题是什么时候记下的、智能体说过同时去做什么，并请它把回答和它后来走的别的路对上（#694）。

### 附上 PDF 和 Word

- 拖入、粘贴或用“添加文件”选择的 PDF 或 `.docx`，会在浏览器里读出文字放进消息。扫描的 PDF 的页面作为图片发送。限制和被省略的内容会在输入框、模型读到的文字和提示里说明。在“文件”标签页里，PDF 按页显示，Word 文档显示为文字（#697）。

### 会话信息、用量与额度

- “信息”标签页的 token 和费用是整个对话的：对话自己的请求，包括 omp 在一旁发出的请求（评审、防护审核模型的）、子代理的和顾问的，经过压缩也不会少。它们取自对话记录，只向 omp 要消息数和工具调用数（#687）。
- “信息”标签页新增**工作时长**：智能体在这个对话上一共工作了多久，包括子代理，实时的和重新加载后的一样（#692）。
- 顶栏的额度涵盖对话花费的每一个提供商：它的模型的、子代理和顾问的，以及防护审核模型的（#688）。
- 用量与费用 ›**按代理类型**把防护审核模型的花费列为单独的 `guard` 类型，带 token 和费用；总数仍是 omp 的，审核模型只算一次（#689）。已知缺口（#706）：`omp stats` 从没读过的那一段记录里的防护请求照样被挪走，所以各类型之和可能与 omp 的总数差几个请求。

### macOS 菜单栏图标

- 图标旁的数字是等你处理的数量：在对话框前等待的对话加上仍待回答的无人值守问题。**✓** 表示自你上次看过之后有一次运行结束了（打开菜单，或把应用窗口带到最前，就算看过）。菜单里多了“N 个问题等待回答”（点它会让窗口打开“问题”页）和“N 个对话已完成”。应用在后台时也有（#705）。

### 防护

- 审核模型的固定指令现在把只读的查看交给你的规则来判断：omp 的用量统计（只读打开的 `~/.omp/stats.db`）、对话记录和 omp 代理文件夹里的其他文件，以及只读取的本地 `/api` 请求。更改应用的设置、批准或防护，以及读取防护自己的文件、设置或规则（包括经由 `/api/guard`）或登录令牌，仍然一律拦下。

### 修复

- 从侧边栏置顶有时会让对话分叉，而不是置顶：置顶由一个重新打开该对话的 omp 完成，而另一个进程正占用该对话时，这个 omp 启动时的写入会落到一个新的同级文件里，被置顶的就成了那个文件。现在由一个从不重新打开该对话的短命 omp 来置顶（#686）。
- “信息”标签页：有子代理或经过压缩的对话，token 和费用的数字不对（#687，见上）。
- 用量与费用：omp 没有给出缓存命中率的行原来显示 0%。现在由这一行的 token 算出，什么都没读的显示 `–`（#689）。
- 无人值守的问题：压缩之后或过了很多轮才到的回答，不再被当成什么都没发生过（#694，见上）。

### 性能

- 用量与费用页立刻显示数据：服务器保存 omp 上一次的 `omp stats` 结果，页面立刻画出来并写明数据截至何时，等 omp 把它自己的数据库更新完，再换成新数据。这里实测，冷启动一次 `omp stats` 要 12.2 秒，几乎都是 omp 在同步对话记录，之后一次 0.1–0.4 秒（#690）。
- 日期和时间改为每种语言、每种格式只建一个格式化器，不再每次调用都新建。性能基准（v0.11.18 对比本版）曾发现首次加载页面和展开长对话历史变慢：每条回复和工具调用上新加的开始时间，以及侧边栏的日期标题，每次都要新建一个格式化器，每次约 15 微秒。修复后，对基准里的对话做 CPU 分析，首次加载（约 85 毫秒对 108 毫秒）和打开长对话（约 77 毫秒对 86 毫秒）花在 JavaScript 上的时间都比 v0.11.18 少。修复后的基准没有发现变慢的指标，但它是**临时**的（机器很忙），而且两次 v0.11.18 的测量之间有漂移，所以单凭它证明不了什么。
